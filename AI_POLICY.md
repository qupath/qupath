# Policy on generative AI 🛑🤖

QuPath is written by humans and we (the core developers) want to keep it that way.

Our policy in a nutshell:

**We don’t accept any AI-generated code contributions to repositories that we maintain.**

If there are exceptions in the future, we’ll specify these clearly (e.g., in a repo’s ReadMe).

Our reasons are personal and considered.

We hope others can respect them, even without sharing them.

Here’s a non-exhaustive list, each held by at least one of us:

- We like writing code. Reviewing code? Not so much. And especially not reviewing the huge volume of code that can be generated with AI.
- If we would give our brains any more ways to avoid hard thinking, we don’t trust that they’d continue to work for us at all. We’d end up poorly ‘maintaining’ a codebase we don’t understand, bereft of our own ideas.
- As a research group, we gravitate towards solving unfashionable problems where there's more need and less competition. If we were to try to solve the same problems as other people, using similar techniques, we lack the self-confidence to believe we’d achieve anything better or faster. So we'd rather leave the generative AI stuff to people who are more enthusiastic about it than us.
- We have a hunch that the widespread use of AI isn’t making the world a better place, and big tech companies might not always have our best interest at heart.
- We also have a sneaking suspicion that large datacenters might not be the best use of the planet’s resources.
- We’ve spent years trying to be responsible in the tools and datasets we use, even when it puts us at a disadvantage (e.g., avoiding dependencies or data that aren’t under open licenses). We’d rather not ignore our misgivings about the legal and ethical uncertainties around AI simply because it’s widespread.

We might change. So might this policy.

But, for now, we're operating under the self-imposed constraint not to introduce AI-generated code into our open source projects.

For more details on contributing, see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
